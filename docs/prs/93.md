# #93 IDE host test can hang

Branch: 93-ide-host-test-can-hang

## Cause

`make check` stalled for 15-46 minutes in `ide_host_with_the_real_compiler_wasm` or
`ide_main_with_the_real_compiler_wasm`. It is not a shared file, port, stdin or an Atomics wait:
the IDE test scripts use none of those at the point of the hang. It is the asynchronous
`WebAssembly.compile(bytes)` in `tests/run.mjs` (`run`), which never settles under CPU load on
Node 23.8.0: V8 hands the compile of the big IDE module to its background threads and the
promise is never resolved.

Evidence:
- 12 pairs of `main_test.mjs`/`host_test.mjs` run concurrently: main hung in 2 (exit 124 of
  `timeout`), host in 1 of the later round. Alone each script takes 0.3 s.
- 24 copies of `main_test.mjs` at once: about 7 of 24 hung, always in the second test
  ("a syntax error in the edited file"), i.e. the second `run` call.
- A hung process: 0 % CPU, main thread idle in the event loop (`NodePlatform::DrainTasks`,
  waiting on a V8 worker task); `sample` showed no JS running. With `console.error` around the
  awaits in `run`, every hung process printed `compile...` and never `compiled`; the
  `instantiate` that follows was never reached.

## Fix

- `tests/run.mjs`: `run` compiles and instantiates synchronously (`new WebAssembly.Module`,
  `new WebAssembly.Instance`); `loadCompiler` uses the new `instantiate` helper, also
  synchronous. No background threads, nothing to hang.
- `compiler/tests/common/mod.rs`: hang guard. `Command::output_guarded()` (stdin null, stdout and
  stderr drained on threads) and `wait_within_deadline(child, deadline)` kill the child after
  300 s and panic with its stdout and stderr. `common::node` uses it too. Every
  `Command::new("node")` (and the `sh` and `tp5c` runs) in `compiler/tests/*.rs` goes through
  it; the `cargo build` calls do not (a cold wasm build may legitimately be long).
  `runner_files.rs` uses `wait_within_deadline` for its piped-stdin child.
- `compiler/tests/ide_host.rs`: `the_hang_guard_kills_a_child_that_never_ends` runs a child that
  prints and then sleeps, with a 1 s deadline, and expects the kill and the captured output.

Not changed: `host/worker.js` (browser; the async instantiate is the right call there) and the
other `.mjs` scripts that use `await WebAssembly.instantiate` for small modules (they never
hung); the guard covers them should they.

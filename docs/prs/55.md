- `docs/abi.md`: `time(what)` contract and the embedded-unit rule. `tests/{lang,neg}/PENDING`:
  the 4 targets removed.

### Decisions

- Contract `time(what)`: 0 takes a reading and returns days since 1970-01-01, 1 and 2 return
  seconds of the day and hundredths of that reading. This replaces plan 4.9's `time() -> i32`:
  an i32 of seconds overflows in 2038, of milliseconds in weeks, and a separate seconds and
  hundredths call could straddle a second boundary. `Delay` (Crt) will need its own contract.
- A user `DOS.PAS` is ignored (measured with TP5: `Marker` from a beside-the-input `DOS.PAS` is
  error 3), so the embedded unit wins, as System does.
- `GetTime` calls `time(0)` into an unused day count: TP5 has no function-call statement.
- Not done: `var Dos: Integer` after `uses Dos` is error 4 in TP5 but accepted here, for user
  units too (units issue, "measured but not pinned"); its test fixture was dropped.
- Other Dos routines are not used by the corpus and are not added.
